ABOUT UPSILVER

ggg2 is 100% USDC-backed silver exposure with up-only tokenomics. The UpSilver Ratio only increases as fees accumulate. 4% fee on mint and redeem.

📊 How Redemption Pricing Works

Mint: Always at silver spot price.
Redeem: You receive the lower of silver spot or contract price.

When contract price > spot (premium exists): redemption pays spot price — the premium stays in the pool, benefiting long-term holders.
When spot > contract price: redemption pays contract price — protecting the backing ratio.
